AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE

How PCDealFinder
may make money.

We believe in being transparent about how PCDealFinder may generate revenue.

OUR BUSINESS MODEL

Affiliate links

PCDealFinder may participate in affiliate, referral or performance-based marketing programs with selected retailers and technology companies.

This means that if you click a qualifying link on PCDealFinder and subsequently make a purchase or complete another qualifying action with a participating retailer, PCDealFinder may receive a commission or referral fee.

YOUR PRICE

Our commission doesn't add a fee.

Where an affiliate relationship exists, the commission is generally paid by the participating retailer or partner. It does not mean that PCDealFinder adds a separate fee to your purchase.

OUR COMMITMENT

We want the best deal to win.

Our goal is to help users compare PC hardware and make better purchasing decisions. An affiliate relationship should not automatically mean that a product is presented as the best option.

Prices, specifications, availability and retailer terms should always be checked before purchasing.

CURRENT STATUS

We're still building.

PCDealFinder is currently developing its retailer and affiliate partnerships. Some links or product information on the current prototype may not be affiliate links.

This disclosure will be updated as commercial partnerships are established.

Transparency matters We will aim to clearly identify qualifying affiliate relationships and keep comparison information as useful and accurate as possible.
