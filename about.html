ABOUT PCDEALFINDER

Better PCs.
Better prices.

PCDealFinder is a South African PC hardware comparison project built to make finding the right components simpler.

WHAT WE DO

PC shopping shouldn't be complicated.

Finding the right PC parts can mean checking multiple retailers, comparing prices, figuring out compatibility and trying to work out whether a particular build is actually good for the games you want to play.

PCDealFinder is being built to bring those decisions together in one place.

OUR GOAL

Find the right part. Find the better deal.

01

Compare

Compare PC components across multiple retailers instead of checking every store individually.

02

Build

Use your budget, games and preferred resolution to work toward a PC configuration that makes sense.

03

Save

Find better-value options and make more informed purchasing decisions.

IMPORTANT

We're building this properly.

PCDealFinder is currently a developing platform. Product prices, availability and retailer information can change, so users should always verify the final price and availability with the retailer before purchasing.

PCDealFinder does not currently sell PC hardware directly. Purchases are completed with the relevant retailer.
